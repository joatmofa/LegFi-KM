LegFi KM is a purpose-built knowledge management platform for financial and legal teams. It gives your organization a single, searchable repository for regulatory guidance, compliance policies, legal precedents, financial records, and audit documentation — with version control and approval workflows built in.

## Why LegFi KM?

Financial and legal teams handle information that is high-stakes, time-sensitive, and heavily regulated. Spreadsheets, email threads, and shared drives create gaps — documents go missing, versions conflict, and audit trails disappear.

LegFi KM solves this by providing:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Centralized Repository" icon="database">
    One home for all regulatory, compliance, and legal knowledge — no more hunting across systems.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Structured Taxonomy" icon="sitemap">
    Organize content with topics and categories that match your compliance framework.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Version Control" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Every change is tracked. Roll back to any previous version with a full change history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audit Trails" icon="shield-check">
    Immutable logs of who accessed, edited, or approved every document — ready for regulators.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Role-Based Access" icon="lock">
    Control who can view, edit, or publish sensitive content at the team and document level.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review Workflows" icon="list-check">
    Route documents for approval before they go live, with full reviewer accountability.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.legfikm.com](https://app.legfikm.com) and configure your organization workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Topics">
    Define the taxonomy that fits your compliance and legal framework — regulatory areas, practice groups, or business units.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Entry">
    Create a knowledge entry, tag it to a topic, and assign it a review owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Add colleagues with appropriate roles so they can contribute, review, and access content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  If you're evaluating LegFi KM for your organization, start with the [Quick Start](/quickstart) guide to see the full workflow end to end in under 10 minutes.
</Tip>
